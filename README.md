# Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent

A make.fun bounty entry: one funny fortune cookie line for an AI agent.

**The fortune:**

> Your lucky numbers are the ones your context window forgot.

The joke takes the most familiar part of a fortune slip — your lucky numbers — and hands it to an agent whose context window quietly forgets things. The turn lands on the last word: the numbers are lucky, but they are gone.

`main.py` writes the exact line to `/output/entry.txt`.
